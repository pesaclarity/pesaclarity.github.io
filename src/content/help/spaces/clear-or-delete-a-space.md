You can clear a space's records and keep the space, or delete the space and its file. Either way, you can keep a locked copy on your phone for 30 days in case you change your mind.

## Clear a space

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Spaces**, then the space.
2. Tap **Clear data**.
3. Under **What to clear**, choose **Everything**, or **A date range** to clear only the transactions, sales and records between two days.
4. Choose whether to **Keep for 30 days**, then confirm.

The space keeps its name, accounts, categories and settings.

## Delete a space

1. Open **Profile › Spaces** and tap the space.
2. Tap **Delete space**.
3. Choose whether to **Keep for 30 days**, and whether to **Also delete the online backup** in the vault and Google Drive.
4. Type the space's name to confirm.

Personal cannot be deleted on its own. To remove everything, see [Delete all your data](/help/privacy-controls/delete-all-your-data/).

## Bring a space back

Open **Profile › Spaces › Recently deleted**. Each copy is kept on your phone, still locked, until its date. Tap **Restore** to put it back in its space, or **Delete these now**.

After 30 days a copy goes for good.
