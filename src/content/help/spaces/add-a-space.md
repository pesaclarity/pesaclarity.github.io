A space keeps one part of your life apart, such as a business, your household or a savings group. Each space has its own accounts, categories, plan, goals and settings, in its own encrypted file on your phone. Nothing is shared or read across spaces.

## Add a space

1. On **Home**, tap the space chip at the top. **Your spaces** opens.
2. Tap **Add a space**.
3. Give it a name, such as Vegetable stall, and choose its kind.
4. Tap **Add a space**.
5. Next, choose which accounts this space reads, such as a bank used only for it.

A business then asks what it sells. See [Set up a business space](/help/business/set-up-a-business/).

## Kinds of space

- **Personal**: your own money and plan.
- **Business**: sales, stock, wages and profit.
- **Household**: shared home costs with family.
- **Savings group**: contributions, payouts and loans.
- **Rental property**: rent in, repairs and profit.
- **Farm**: inputs, labour and harvest sales.
- **Something else**: a project or anything to keep apart.

Each kind starts with its own categories, such as rent collected, repairs and caretaker for a rental. Home for a space that is not Personal leads with its own figure, such as profit this month for a business.

## Which money goes where

An account's messages land in the space that reads it. When one line is used for both, such as one M-Pesa number for you and your stall, move each payment by hand. See [Move transactions to another space](/help/spaces/move-transactions/).

## Switch spaces

Tap the space chip at the top of Home and choose a space. To choose which one the app opens in, tap **Open the app in** under **Your spaces**: **The last space you used**, a space you pick, or **Ask each time**.

All spaces use the same app lock, and the same sign-in.
