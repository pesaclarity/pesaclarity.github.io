When a payment lands in the wrong space, such as a sale paid to your personal M-Pesa number, move it to the right one. Transactions are never edited, only recategorised or moved.

## Move one transaction

1. In **Activity**, tap the transaction.
2. Tap **Move to another space**.
3. Under **Move to**, tap the space. The app says **Moved to** that space.

## Move several at once

1. In **Activity**, press and hold a transaction. Tap the others you want to move too.
2. Tap **Move**.
3. Under **Move to**, choose the space.
4. Under **Category there**, choose a category from that space, or **Match each one**.
5. Tap **Move**, with how many you chose, such as **Move 3 transactions**. The app says where they went.

## What moves

- They move, not copy: the space they leave will not have them any more.
- Only the transactions go. Payees stay where they were.
- Each one goes with its account, matched by bank or name in the other space.
- Reading the same message again does not bring it back.

## A line used for both

In a business, **Profile › Business or personal** sets each account to the business, **Personal** or **Both**. For a line used for both, business Home asks about each recent payment: **Was this for the business?** Tap **Business sale** (or **Business cost** for money out) or **Personal**. Tap **Always do this for** that person to have their payments sorted from then on.
