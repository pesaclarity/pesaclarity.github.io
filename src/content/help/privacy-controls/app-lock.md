App lock asks for a 6-digit PIN, or your fingerprint, to open Pesa Clarity. With it on, the key to your data is released only after you unlock the app.

## Turn on app lock

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right.
2. Under **Security**, switch on **App lock**.
3. Choose a 6-digit PIN, then enter it again. The app says **App lock is on.**
4. To unlock with your fingerprint, switch on **Fingerprint unlock**. If the phone has no fingerprint yet, add one in the phone's settings first.

## Choose when it locks

Under **Security**, tap **Lock after** and choose **Immediately**, 30 seconds, 1 minute or 5 minutes after you leave the app. The usual is 30 seconds.

To change your PIN, tap **Change PIN** and enter your current PIN first.

## Why it matters

Your data sits in an encrypted database on your phone, and its key is held by the phone's secure hardware.

- With app lock on, the key is released only after you unlock with your PIN, fingerprint or Face ID.
- Without app lock, the key is released when the app opens.

After too many wrong PINs, the app makes you wait before you try again.

If you forget your PIN, reinstall the app and restore from your backup. See [Restore a backup](/help/backups/restore-a-backup/).

## Hide your amounts

Tap the eye on any screen to hide or show every amount in the app. To start with amounts hidden each time the app opens, switch on **Hide balances on open** under **Security**.

Selling and forms always show amounts, so you can serve a customer.
