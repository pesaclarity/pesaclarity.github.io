Your transactions, payees and debts stay on your phone. Three switches decide whether anything else leaves it to help the app, and you can change them any time.

## Change your choices

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Privacy**.
2. Under **Your choices**, switch each one on or off.

## The three choices

- **Help improve the app**: on unless you switch it off. Your phone may send clipped, noised updates to the categoriser, combined with other people's before we can see them. Never your transactions.
- **Contribute to licensed models**: off unless you switch it on. Pattern-only models, privacy-protected.
- **Send crash reports**: off unless you switch it on. Anonymous, with no financial details.

Ask is never part of any of these. Your questions and answers stay on your phone.

## What leaves your phone anyway

- **Exchange rates**: to count money in other currencies, the app downloads today's rates. The request carries only your base currency's code. You can set your own rates instead.
- **The update check**: once a day, the app asks our server whether a newer version or a notice is waiting. It carries only the app's version, which build it is and the platform. No account, device or install id.
- **Sign-in and backups**, only if you use them. Backups are encrypted on your phone before upload, so we cannot read them.

Pesa Clarity has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

## Read the policy

In **Privacy**, tap **Read the full privacy policy**, or **Email me a copy**. The policy is also [on this site](/privacy/). When it changes, the app shows what changed and asks you to read it before you carry on.
