**Delete all my data** removes every space, backup setting and record on your phone. If you like, it also deletes your online backups and your sign-in. The app then starts again from setup.

## Delete everything

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Spaces**.
2. Tap **Delete all my data**.
3. Under **Also**, choose whether to **Delete my online backups**, every space's copy in the vault and Drive, and **Delete my sign-in**, your email and devices on the server.
4. Enter your app lock PIN. Without app lock, type DELETE.
5. Tap **Delete everything**.

It deletes at once: nothing is kept for 30 days, and it cannot be undone.

## Delete only your account

To keep your data on the phone but remove your account, open **Profile** while signed in and tap **Delete account**. It removes your account, sign-in records and vault backups from our server. Your data on this phone stays, and so do backups saved to your own Google Drive or SD card.

## Delete one space

To delete one space and keep the rest, see [Clear or delete a space](/help/spaces/clear-or-delete-a-space/).
