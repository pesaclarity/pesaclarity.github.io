Each account's balance comes from its messages. When the balance one message reports and the money in and out since the last one disagree, the app shows where the gap is and helps you close it.

## Where you see it

- On **Home**, a line under the top card says, for example, **M-Pesa doesn't add up**, with how much is unaccounted for and since when. Tap **Check**.
- Or tap **Profile** at the top right of Home, then **Balances**, then **Check all balances**.

Both open **Balance check**. It says how many things there are to fix, and lists the accounts that already match.

## Fix it

1. Each gap shows its likely reason, such as **Counted twice**, **Might be in the wrong account** or **Probably a message we missed**.
2. When the app knows the fix, the gap offers it, such as **Remove the copy** or **Move it to** the right account. The button at the bottom, **Fix it** or **Fix all**, does every fix it knows at once.
3. Otherwise, tap the gap to see it: the two balances, what happened between them, and the payment that is likely missing.
4. Tap **Add what's missing** to add a payment the app never saw. It goes in that account as a normal transaction, so your plan and insights count it. Choose what it was and tap **Add it**.
5. If nothing is missing, tap **It's just a correction** instead.

If a message is still waiting in **Messages to check**, the gap may be that message. Tap **Check this message** and check it first.

## Corrections

A correction is not income or spending, so it never changes your plan, insights or tax.

Until you add what is missing or add a correction, the gap is kept as not yet sorted, so the balance still matches the message.
