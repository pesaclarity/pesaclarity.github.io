When you know what an account really holds, type it in. The app records the difference as a correction, which you can undo.

## Set the balance

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Balances**.
2. Tap the account.
3. Tap **Set the balance**.
4. Type what the account holds now. If you owe Fuliza, choose **Owes Fuliza** and type what you owe.
5. Tap **Set the balance**. The app says **Balance set.**

The difference is recorded as a balance correction and shows in Activity. A correction is not income or spending, so it never changes your plan, insights or tax.

## Undo it

Open the account again in **Balances** and tap **Undo the last correction**.

## Cash and accounts with no messages

For cash, or an account whose messages the app does not read, tap **Add an account** in **Balances**. Give it a name, a type and what it holds now.
