Every backup is locked with your passphrase on your phone before it goes anywhere, so only you can open it. Choose a passphrase, save your recovery key, then choose where backups go.

## Set up backups

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Backup and restore**.
2. Tap **Set up**.
3. Type a passphrase of at least 8 characters. A short sentence works well. Type it again and tap **Continue**.
4. The app shows your recovery key. It opens your backups if you forget your passphrase, and it is shown only now. Tap **Save as PDF**, **Print** or **Share as text** to keep a copy somewhere safe.
5. Tick **I have saved my recovery key somewhere safe** and tap **Finish**.

## Choose where backups go

Under **Back up to**, switch on one or more places:

- **Phone or SD card** (on iPhone, **Files on this iPhone**): a `.pcbackup` file in a folder you choose. It works offline.
- **My Google Drive**: your own Drive account. Only in the Google Play build.
- **Pesa Clarity vault**: encrypted copies on our server. It needs you to sign in, and shows **Available when the vault service opens** until then. The vault keeps your latest 10 backups.

Then tap **Back up now**. The app says **Backed up.**, and the card at the top says when.

## Share a copy

Tap **Share a copy** to send the encrypted file anywhere you like, such as your email. It opens only with your passphrase or recovery key.

## What a backup holds

- Each space is backed up in its own file.
- Ask's conversations are left out.
- Nobody else can open a backup, not even us. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, the backup cannot be opened.

To change them, tap **Change passphrase and recovery key**.
