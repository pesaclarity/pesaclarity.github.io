A backup opens with your passphrase or your recovery key. Your current data stays as it is until the restore opens and checks out.

## Restore a backup

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Backup and restore**.
2. Under **Restore from**, choose where the backup is: your backup folder, **Google Drive**, **Vault**, or **A file on this phone** and **Choose file**.
3. Pick the backup.
4. Type your passphrase. If you forgot it, tap **Use my recovery key** and type that instead.
5. Tap **Restore**. The app says **Restored.**, with the date your data is from.

A restore replaces only the space you have open, and lists only that space's backups. To restore another space, open it first.

## Move to a new phone

1. On your old phone, tap **Back up now**, or **Share a copy** to send the file to yourself.
2. Install Pesa Clarity on the new phone and go through setup.
3. Open **Profile › Backup and restore** and restore the backup, as above.

A backup made in the Google Play build restores in the F-Droid build, and the other way round.

## If it does not open

- **That passphrase or recovery key does not open this backup**: check it and try again.
- **This file is not a Pesa Clarity backup**: choose a `.pcbackup` file.
- **This backup is damaged or incomplete**: your data is unchanged. Try another backup.

## Forgot your app lock PIN?

Reinstall the app, then restore your backup with your passphrase or recovery key. Reinstalling removes the data on the phone, so keep a recent backup.
