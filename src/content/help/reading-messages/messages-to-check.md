When a bank or wallet words a message in a way the app has not seen, the message waits in **Messages to check**. Check it once, fix anything the app got wrong, and the app reads the next one like it on its own.

## Find the messages to check

When messages are waiting, a card on **Home** says so, such as **3 messages to check**. If money that came in is also waiting for a name, the card shows the total instead, such as **KES 12,400 not yet sorted**, with what makes it up under it.

Tap the card to open **Messages to check**. Each message shows the bank or wallet, when it came, the start of its text and what the app suggests, such as **Suggested: Money out · 1,500 to JANE DOE**.

## Check one message

1. Tap the message. **Check this message** opens, with the message's text and the parts the app read marked in colour.
2. Choose what it is: **Money out**, **Money in**, or **My accounts** for money you moved between your own accounts.
3. Under **Suggested · tap to change**, look at **Amount**, **Paid to** or **Received from**, **Date** and **Receipt**. A row the app is unsure of says **Check this**. Tap a row to change it, type the right value and tap **Save**.
4. Leave **Read messages like this the same way** on, so the app reads the next message with this wording without asking.
5. Tap **Add a transaction**. The app says **Added to Activity.**

If the message is not about money, such as an offer or a notice, tap **It's not money** instead.

## What the app keeps

The message's text stays encrypted on your phone and is deleted once you check it.

With **Read messages like this the same way** on, the app saves the message's layout on your phone, for that bank or wallet only. The layout keeps the wording with the amount, payee, date and other details taken out, so it holds nothing about your money.

## Tell us about new wording

If a bank changed its messages, tell us, so everyone's app reads them. Rewrite the message by hand with made-up names, numbers and amounts, then [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/pesaclarity/ujumbe-parsers/issues).

> **Never send a real message.** Use names like JANE DOE, numbers like 0700\*\*\*001 and round amounts.
