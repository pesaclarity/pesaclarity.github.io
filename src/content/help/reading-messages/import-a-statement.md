A statement adds your history from before you installed Pesa Clarity. The PDF is read on your phone and stays there. For now the app reads M-Pesa statements; bank statements are coming.

## Import a statement

1. Save your full M-Pesa statement as a PDF, as Safaricom sent it, from the M-Pesa app.
2. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Import a statement**.
3. Tap **Choose a PDF** and pick one or more statements.
4. If the statement has a password, type it once. It opens the file and is then forgotten.
5. The app reads the statement and shows what it added. It says **Statement imported** when it is done.

## What the app checks

- Transactions already read from your SMS are matched and counted once.
- The result is checked against the statement's closing balance, and any gap is shown.
- A statement you imported before is recognised, so nothing is added twice.

## If it does not work

- **This PDF is a scanned picture**: the app needs the statement as the M-Pesa app saved it. Download it again from there and choose that file.
- **That password did not open the file**: check the password and try again.
- **We could not find M-Pesa transactions here**: the file is not an M-Pesa statement.

Statement import is in the Google Play build and on iPhone. The F-Droid build does not have it. See [Which download is for me?](/help/getting-started/which-download/)
