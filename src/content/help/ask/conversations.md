Each space keeps its own Ask conversations, encrypted on your phone, for as long as you choose. Backups leave them out, and they never leave the phone.

## Find a conversation

1. In Ask, tap the **Conversations** button at the top. They are grouped under **TODAY**, **THIS WEEK** and **EARLIER**.
2. Tap **Search conversations** to find one by its words.
3. Tap **New conversation** to start again.

Each conversation is named from your first question. To change it, tap **More** at the top of the conversation, then **Rename**.

## Update the numbers

A saved answer keeps the numbers of the day you asked, and says **Numbers as of** that day. Tap **Update** for today's numbers. For the whole conversation, tap **More**, then **Update all numbers**.

## Ask settings

Open **Profile › Ask**, or in a conversation tap **More**, then **Ask settings**. The settings are for the space you have open.

- **Show Ask**: the bar and the button on Home. Switch it off to hide Ask in this space.
- **Save conversations**: on this phone, in each space. Switch it off to keep none.
- **Keep them for**: **Until I delete**, **1 year**, **90 days**, **30 days** or **7 days**. Older ones are removed.
- **Charts in answers**: when an answer has a trend.

## Delete conversations

1. To delete one, open it, tap **More**, then **Delete conversation**, and confirm.
2. To delete them all, open **Ask settings** and tap **Delete all conversations**. Every conversation, and every word Ask learned, in that space goes from this phone. Other spaces keep theirs.
