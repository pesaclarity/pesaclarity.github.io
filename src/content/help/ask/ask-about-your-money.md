Ask answers questions about your money in your own words, such as "How much did I spend on food last month?". Small models inside the app read your question and word the answer, on your phone. Every number comes from your own records.

## Ask a question

1. Tap **Ask about your money**, the bar above the tabs. When you scroll Home, the bar slides away and the Ask button beside Profile takes its place.
2. Type your question, such as **How much came in this week?**, and tap **Ask**. To start, you can also tap one of the questions under **TRY ASKING**.
3. The answer shows with its figures. Where it can, it offers a next step, such as **See the payments**.
4. To narrow it down, type a follow-up, such as "and only Naivas?". The follow-up changes only what it names; what you are asking about shows above the box under **Asking about**.

If Ask is not sure what you mean, it asks, such as **And for when?**. Your answer becomes a shortcut for next time in that space, such as "chakula" meaning Food.

## What you can ask

- What you spent, on what and with whom, such as **How much did I spend on food last month?**
- What came in, and from where.
- **What is safe to spend?**
- What you still owe, and bills coming up.
- In a business: **How much did I sell today?** or **What sold most this week?**

The figures are the same ones the screens show: balances as Balances shows them, and safe to spend as Home shows it.

## What Ask keeps to itself

- Nothing is sent anywhere. Ask works without internet.
- Ask answers only from the space you have open. In a business space, it asks about the business.
- Ask has no federated learning. **Help improve the app** never includes anything from Ask.

To keep or delete conversations, see [Ask's conversations and settings](/help/ask/conversations/).
