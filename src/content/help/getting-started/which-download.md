There are two Android builds: one with Google services, and the F-Droid build without them. Each comes for newer phones and for older 32-bit phones. Almost everything is in both; the few differences are below.

The app is not in any store yet. Every build comes from the app's [releases on GitHub](https://github.com/pesaclarity/pesaclarity-app/releases), and the [Install on Android](/install/) page links each one.

## Choose a build

1. **For most phones** is the build made for Google Play. Start here.
2. **F-Droid build** carries no Google services or other closed code. Choose it if you prefer software that is open all the way through.
3. If your phone is older or runs Android Go, choose the 32-bit download of the same build: **For older phones**, or **F-Droid, older phones**.

## What differs

The Google Play build can also:

- sign in with **Continue with Google**, as well as with an email code;
- back up to **My Google Drive**, once the app shows it as ready rather than coming soon;
- import a past M-Pesa statement from **Profile › Import a statement**.

The F-Droid build:

- signs in with an email code only;
- backs up to the Pesa Clarity vault, your phone or an SD card;
- has no statement import;
- is the only build that asks, now and then, whether you would like to give a small gift. Giving never unlocks anything: every feature is free in every build.

If the app says accounts arrive in a later update, sign-in and the vault are not open yet. The app works fully without an account.

## Moving between builds

Both builds keep your data in the same format, so a backup made in one restores in the other. See [Restore a backup](/help/backups/restore-a-backup/).

## On iPhone

A test build installs with SideStore or AltStore, signed with your own Apple ID. See [On iPhone](/install/#iphone).
