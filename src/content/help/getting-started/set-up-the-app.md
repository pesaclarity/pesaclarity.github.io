Setup takes a few minutes. You choose your currency, say how money usually comes to you, tick your accounts and let the app read their messages. You need no account to start.

## Go through setup

1. Open Pesa Clarity and tap **Get started**.
2. Under **Which currency do you count in?**, the app suggests your phone's region's currency. Tap **Continue with KES**, or choose another.
3. Under **How does money usually come to you?**, choose all that fit, such as **Salary**, **Business or hustle** or **Family or sponsor support**, and tap **Continue**. The app plans around them.
4. Under **Which accounts do you use?**, tick the banks and wallets you use, such as M-Pesa, and tap **Scan my messages**.
5. Read what the app keeps, then tap **Allow access to messages**. Android asks whether Pesa Clarity may read and receive SMS.
6. The app reads your messages and shows a first look at your money. Tap **Go to Home**.

If you would rather not share your messages yet, tap **Continue without messages**. You can still add transactions by hand.

## What the app reads

Only messages from the accounts you ticked are read. Your personal chats stay private.

From each message the app keeps the amount, date, fee and who you paid, then deletes the message text. Everything stays on your phone, in an encrypted database.

## Change your answers later

Each answer can be changed in **Profile**, the round button at the top right of Home:

- **Base currency** for the currency you count in.
- **How money comes in** for how money comes to you.
- **My accounts** for which banks and wallets are read.

If Android says the SMS setting is restricted, or you use a Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO phone, see [Install on Android](/install/).
