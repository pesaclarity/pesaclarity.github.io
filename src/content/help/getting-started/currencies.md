Your totals, budgets and goals show in one base currency, such as KES. Each transaction keeps the currency it moved in, and is converted at the rate of its day.

## Change your base currency

1. On **Home**, tap **Profile** at the top right, then **Base currency**.
2. Choose the currency you count in. Search by name or code, such as USD.
3. The app says what moves to the new currency. Tap **Switch to** with its code, such as **Switch to USD**.

Your plan, goals, debts, money you lent and subscriptions are converted once, at today's rate. Transactions keep their own currency.

## How amounts show

- **Activity** shows each payment in the currency it moved in, with the base amount under it, such as **≈ USD 193.50**.
- Bank messages are read in the currency they print. A message that prints none is in its bank or wallet's usual currency.
- Two accounts at the same bank, such as one in KES and one in USD, are told apart by the account number in their messages.
- **Profile › My accounts** lists each account and its currency.

## Rates

The app loads today's rates from a public rates service once you are online, at most every 12 hours. The request carries only your base currency's code. The first rates of each day are kept, so a transaction always converts at the rates of its day and later rates do not change it.

- In **Profile › Base currency**, tap **Set my own rate** for a currency. Rates you set always win.
- Open a payment to see the rate it used and where it came from. Tap **Use the rate I got** to enter the rate you really got.

## Currency converter

In **Profile › Base currency**, tap **Currency converter**. It converts any amount between any two currencies, and works offline with the last rates.
