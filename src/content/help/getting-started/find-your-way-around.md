Personal is the space for your own money, and the one the app starts in. Five tabs at the bottom hold it: **Home**, **Activity**, **Plan**, **Goals** and **Insights**.

## The five tabs

1. **Home** leads with what is safe to spend today, then what is left this month, bills coming up and your money health score.
2. **Activity** lists every transaction, newest first. Search it, or filter by account and type.
3. **Plan** splits your money across bills, debts, savings and living, and shows spending by category.
4. **Goals** holds what you are saving for, and **Debts**: your debt-free date, the payoff order and the Fuliza tracker.
5. **Insights** shows what changed this month and where your money goes.

## Profile

**Profile** is the round button at the top right of Home. It holds your accounts and balances, base currency, time zone, look, backups, privacy and app lock.

## Safe to spend today

The figure on Home is what you can spend today and still cover your bills.

- With a salary and a plan, it comes from your monthly plan.
- Otherwise it takes the money you have, less the bills due before your next money and 10% kept back, and spreads it over the days until then. It is never more than you usually spend on a day like today.

You told the app how money comes to you during setup. To change it, open **Profile › How money comes in**.

## Hide your amounts

Tap the eye on any screen to hide or show every amount. Selling and forms always show amounts. To start with them hidden, see [App lock and hiding amounts](/help/privacy-controls/app-lock/).

## Other spaces

The chip at the top of Home switches between your spaces, such as a business or a household. Each one has its own records. See [Add a space](/help/spaces/add-a-space/).
