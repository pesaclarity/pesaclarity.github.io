**People** shows what each customer owes you, and what you owe suppliers. Send a friendly reminder, and record payments as they come.

## Remind a customer

1. Open **People**. **Customers** shows what they owe in all, and what is over two weeks old.
2. Tap **Remind** beside the customer.
3. Pick a message, such as **Friendly** or **Due today**. The name, amount and due date fill in on their own. Change the words for this one if you like.
4. Add their number, or tap **Choose from contacts**. The app gets only the contact you pick.
5. WhatsApp or your SMS app opens with the message filled in. You tap send.

To go through everyone at once, tap **Remind all who are due**.

## Record a payment

When a customer pays, tap **Got paid** on **Today** and choose them. Their payment comes off their oldest credit first. An M-Pesa or bank payment waits for its message, like a sale.

## A customer's statement

Tap a customer to see what they owe and every credit sale and payment. To send it, tap **Share the statement (PDF)**, or **Print statement**.

## Suppliers

Switch to **Suppliers** to see what the business owes, what is due this week and what was paid this month. Buy on credit with **Bought** and **Pay later**, and record what you pay with **Paid**.

## Reminder messages

**People › Messages** edits your reminder messages, the app they open in, and a daily notification of who is due.
