Tap **+**, pick what you sold or type the amount, then choose how they paid. Cash goes into the drawer at once. M-Pesa and bank sales match their payment message on their own.

## Sell

1. On **Today** or **Money**, tap the red **+**. **Sell** opens.
2. Tap an item's tile to add one. Tap it again for more. For something not in Stock, tap **Other sale** and type the amount.
3. Tap **Pay**.
4. Under **How are they paying?**, choose **Cash**, **M-Pesa**, **Bank**, **On credit** or **Split**. For cash, type the cash given to see the change.
5. Tap **Done**. The sale is saved, with its receipt.

Tap **Share receipt** to send it as a picture, a PDF or text, or **Print** to print it.

## How each way to pay works

- **Cash** counts at once.
- **M-Pesa** and **Bank** wait for the payment message. When the amount agrees within three hours, the sale matches it on its own.
- **On credit** asks who will pay and when. What they owe shows in **People**, and the sale counts when you make it.
- **Split** takes part now and the rest another way, or on credit.

## A sale still waiting

Today lists sales still **Waiting for payment**. Tap one to sort it:

- **Choose payments**, when it came in parts or as another amount. Tick the messages that paid it.
- **It was paid in cash**.
- **Delete it**, if the sale did not happen.

## Open tabs

For a table or a person who pays at the end, tap **Add to a tab** beside Pay. Each round is kept with its time. When they are done, open the tab and tap **Pay and close** with its total. Stock leaves only when a tab is paid.
