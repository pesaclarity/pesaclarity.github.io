At the end of the day, count the cash in the drawer and compare it with what the records say it should hold: the day's start, plus cash in, less cash out.

## Close the day

1. On **Today** or **Money**, tap **Close the day**.
2. **Should be in the drawer** shows what the records expect.
3. Count the cash and type it under **Cash you counted**.
4. If they agree, the app says **The drawer matches the records.**
5. Tap **Close the day**.

To keep a copy, tap **Print the day's summary**.

## When the drawer does not match

The app says how much more or less there is than the records. If you know what was missed, add the sale or cost with **+** first. Otherwise, under **The difference**, choose:

- **Keep a note**: the difference is kept with the close, and your records stay as they are.
- **Book a cash difference**: the difference goes into the records.

A difference is never booked unless you choose to.

## Money in with no record

If money came in today with no record yet, the close lists it. Tap **Match** and choose what it was: **A sale**, a customer paying what they owe, or **Other income**.

## Open tabs

Tabs still open show on the close, with **See the tabs**, so you can settle them first.

Each close is kept, and **Insights** counts how often the drawer matched.
