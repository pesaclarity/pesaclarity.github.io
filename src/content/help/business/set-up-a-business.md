A business space keeps the business's money apart from yours, with its own tabs: **Today**, **Money**, **People**, **Stock** and **Insights**. Tell it what you sell and what the business has today, and its records start right.

## Set it up

1. Add a space of the kind **Business**, and choose the accounts it reads. See [Add a space](/help/spaces/add-a-space/).
2. Under **What do you sell?**, choose **Products**, **Services** or **Both**, and tap **Continue**. This sets up the right screens. You can change it later.
3. Under **Start your records**, type the **Cash in the drawer**, and what each M-Pesa, till and bank account holds today. Where a message showed the balance, it is already filled in.
4. Add **Customers who owe you** and **Suppliers you owe** now, or later.
5. Tap **Start**.

If you skip this, Home shows **Set up your business records** until it is done.

## What each choice gives you

- **Products**: selling by item, stock and restocking, customers and suppliers, and profit.
- **Services**: clients, your services and rates, income and costs, and Pay yourself. No stock. The tabs show **Clients** and **Services** in place of People and Stock.
- **Both**: everything for products and services, with their profit shown apart.

## The tabs

- **Today** leads with sales today, by how people paid, against a usual day of that weekday. Its quick records are **Bought**, **Got paid**, **Paid** and **Other record**.
- **Money** is the cash book: what Cash, M-Pesa and the bank hold, and each day's money in and out.
- **People** shows what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers.
- **Stock** lists what you sell, with what is left and what is running low.
- **Insights** shows profit, how people pay, best sellers and tax estimates.

## Pay yourself

Money you take for yourself is not a cost. On **Today**, tap **Other record**, then **Pay yourself**. It leaves the business and comes into Personal.

## A late night

If you are open past midnight, set **Profile › Day ends at** to 2, 4 or 6 am. Sales today and **Close the day** then run to that hour.
